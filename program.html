Core Pillar Tracks 核心议题板块

Each track blends technical depth, governance design, and real-world implementation discussion. 每个议题板块都同时覆盖技术深度、治理设计与现实落地路径。

  • Track A: Technical Safety 议题A：技术安全

    Focusing on AI autonomy, catastrophic risk prevention, and standards for deepfake detection and data privacy. 关注AI自主化风险、灾难性风险防范，以及深度伪造检测与数据隐私标准。

  • Track B: Governance Architecture 议题B：治理架构

    Comparative analysis of global AI acts and Hong Kong's strategic positioning as a policy bridge. 全球AI法案对比分析，以及香港作为政策桥梁的战略定位。

  • Track C: Education & Society 议题C：教育与社会

    Exploring interdisciplinary digital literacy, research integrity, and the long-term impact on labor markets. 探索跨学科数字素养、科研诚信以及对劳动力市场的长期影响。
