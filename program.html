Core Pillar Tracks 核心议题板块

  • Track A: Technical Security and Risk Prevention in the Era of Human-Machine Symbiosis 支柱一：人机共生时代的技术安全与风险防范

    This session focuses on the core technological risks in the era of human-machine symbiosis. With the widespread adoption of large language models such as ChatGPT, GPT-4 and Gemini, alongside the rapid advancement of AI Agents, AI systems are evolving from passive tools into active participants in social operations. A host of challenges have become increasingly prominent, including data privacy breaches, algorithmic bias and discrimination, deepfake abuse and disinformation dissemination, as well as AI-giarism and copyright disputes. From dual technical and policy perspectives, participating experts will explore the development of explainable and accountable AI systems, formulate technical standards and detection mechanisms for AI safety boundaries, and put forward targeted guidelines for the secure deployment of generative AI in critical sectors including healthcare, education and finance. Researcher Zeng Yi from the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Co-Chair of the AI Safety and Governance Committee of the World Internet Conference, will present his latest research on AI safety red lines and catastrophic risk prevention. Turing Award Laureate Professor Yoshua Bengio and Professor Stuart Russell from the University of California, Berkeley will discuss the concentration of economic and political power, democratic resilience, and responsibility-based regulatory frameworks in the AGI era based on cutting-edge AI safety research. Beyond theoretical discussion, the in-depth dialogue on technological safety aims to deliver actionable technical standards and industrial norms, providing a practical safety roadmap for AI adoption by regional governments and enterprises. 本板块将聚焦人机共生时代的核心技术风险。随着 ChatGPT、GPT-4、Gemini 等大语言模型的普及应用，以及人工智能智能体（AI Agent）的高速发展，人工智能系统正由被动工具转变为社会活动的主动参与者。数据隐私泄露、算法偏见与歧视、深度伪造技术滥用、虚假信息泛滥、AI 抄袭与版权纠纷等安全治理问题愈发凸显。 与会专家将结合技术实践与政策治理两大维度，探讨如何搭建可解释、可问责的人工智能体系，制定人工智能安全底线的技术规范与风险检测方案，并针对生成式 AI 在医疗、教育、金融等关键民生领域的合规安全应用，提供实践指导与落地建议。 中国科学院自动化研究所曾毅研究员，担任世界互联网大会人工智能安全与治理专业委员会联合主席，将围绕人工智能安全红线与极端灾难性风险防控，分享前沿研究成果。图灵奖得主约书亚・本吉奥（Yoshua Bengio）、加州大学伯克利分校斯图尔特・拉塞尔（Stuart Russell）两位学者，将立足全球顶尖 AI 安全研究视角，剖析通用人工智能（AGI）时代下经济与政治权力集中化、社会民主韧性建设等议题，探索以责任为核心的人工智能监管体系。 本次关于技术安全的研讨，不止于理论研究，更着重输出可落地、可执行的技术准则与行业规范，为地方政府及市场企业的人工智能规模化应用，提供完善的安全发展指引。

  • Track B: Global AI Governance Framework and Hong Kong’s Role 支柱二：全球AI治理框架与香港角色

    This session systematically compares major global AI governance frameworks and explores the strengths and limitations of diverse legal traditions and governance models. The EU AI Act adopts a risk-based tiered regulatory model; China implements a hierarchical governance framework and has issued special regulations including the Interim Measures for the Administration of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services. Singapore pursues a “light-touch” regulatory approach that combines industry self-regulation with government guidance. These distinct governance paths reflect different trade-offs between innovation and security across jurisdictions. The discussion will focus on how Hong Kong, leveraging its unique institutional advantages under One Country, Two Systems, its common law system, pool of international talent, and role as a “super connector”, can foster a flexible, forward-looking AI regulatory environment and serve as a critical bridge for AI governance between mainland China and the international community. Professor Xue Lan from Tsinghua University will share global insights as a member of the United Nations High-Level Advisory Body on AI Governance, while Professor Simon Chesterman from the National University of Singapore will offer an analytical framework from the perspectives of international law and comparative law. Endowed with a unique strategic position, Hong Kong has the potential to become a pilot ground and convergence hub for global AI governance rules, contributing to the development of an inclusive international AI governance system. 本板块将系统比较全球主要AI治理框架，探讨不同法律传统与治理模式的优势与局限。欧盟《人工智能法 案》采用基于风险的分类监管模式；中国采取分层治理框架，先后出台《生成式人工智能服务管理暂行办 法》等专项法规；新加坡则推行"轻触式"监管路径，强调行业自律与政府引导相结合。这些不同的治理路径 反映了各国在创新与安全之间的不同权衡。 重点研讨香港如何在"一国两制"的独特制度优势下，依托普通法体系、国际化人才储备及"超级联系人"角 色，打造灵活且具前瞻性的AI监管环境，成为连接中国内地与国际社会的AI治理桥梁。清华大学薛澜教授将 分享其作为联合国AI治理高级别咨询机构成员的全球视野，NUS Simon Chesterman教授将从国际法与比较 法的角度提供分析框架。香港的独特地位使其有潜力成为全球AI治理规则的试验田和交汇点，为构建包容性 的国际AI治理体系贡献力量。

  • Track C: Scientific and Humanistic Literacy & Digital Education in the Era of Human-Machine Symbiosis 支柱三：人机共生时代的科技人文素养与数字教育

    This session explores how to systematically enhance the scientific and humanistic literacy of the general public and students through the Interdisciplinary Digital Literacy Program (IDLP) in the era of human-machine symbiosis. With the widespread popularization of AI technologies, technical education alone is no longer sufficient to address future challenges. The discussion covers the curriculum design and evaluation system for AI literacy education, AI ethics education from humanities and social science perspectives, the cultivation of a new generation of AI talents with critical thinking and ethical awareness, as well as the profound impacts of AI on the job market and social structures and corresponding coping strategies. In the era of human-machine symbiosis, human creativity, empathy and moral judgment will become increasingly vital. The education system needs to shift from traditional knowledge transmission to fostering students’ ability to collaborate with AI, while maintaining independent thinking and critical analysis skills. The Interdisciplinary Digital Literacy Program (IDLP) breaks down the barriers between liberal arts and science, deeply integrates technical education with humanistic care, and offers a new pathway for cultivating interdisciplinary talents adaptable to future society. 本板块将探讨在人机共生时代，如何通过跨学科数字素养项目（IDLP），系统提升公众及学生的科技人文 素养。随着AI技术的普及，单纯的技术教育已不足以应对未来的挑战。议题涵盖AI素养教育的课程设计与评 估体系、人文社科视角下的AI伦理教育、培养具备批判性思维与伦理意识的新一代AI人才，以及AI对就业市 场与社会结构的深层影响及应对策略。 在人机共生时代，人类的创造力、同理心和道德判断力将变得更加重要。教育体系需要从传统的知识传授转 向培养学生与AI协同工作的能力，同时保持独立思考和批判性分析的能力。跨学科数字素养项目（IDLP） 在打破文理科的界限，将技术教育与人文关怀深度融合，为培养适应未来社会的复合型人才提供新路径。
