Strategic Vision

Generative AI is rapidly transforming research, governance, public communication, and cultural production. This conference convenes world-leading scholars, policymakers, and industry leaders to build actionable, globally representative governance pathways balancing innovation, safety, and human values.

  • Global Window of Opportunity

    With the EU AI Act rollout, UN global AI dialogue mechanisms, and emerging frameworks across Asia, 2026 is a critical moment to align regional practices with international governance standards.

  • Hong Kong as Connector

    Leveraging its common law system and "super-connector" role, Hong Kong can bridge East-West policy approaches and pilot forward-looking, context-aware AI governance.

  • Anniversary Impact

    As an HKUST 35th Anniversary Feature Impact Case, the conference amplifies the university's leadership in both frontier AI innovation and human-centered governance.

Three Core Pillars

The program is built around an integrated triad to deliver both academic depth and policy relevance.

  • Technical Safety & Risk Prevention

    Addressing autonomy risks, privacy leakage, deepfakes, algorithmic bias, AI plagiarism, and copyright disputes with practical standards for explainable and accountable AI systems.

  • Global Governance & Hong Kong's Role

    Comparative analysis of EU, UN, China, and ASEAN governance models to define Hong Kong's strategic positioning as a policy bridge under One Country, Two Systems.

  • Humanistic Literacy & Digital Education

    Advancing interdisciplinary AI literacy through digital education models that strengthen ethics, critical thinking, creativity, and social responsibility in future talent.

Featured Activities

Designed to activate academia, industry, and society through high-impact, participatory initiatives.

  • Global AI Popular Science Book Awards: worldwide nominations and public voting for the Top 10 list.
  • China's Safest AI Agent Enterprise Challenge: expert screening and live final showcase with audience voting.
  • Young Entrepreneurs Ethics Forum: founder stories on building trustworthy, responsible AI products.
