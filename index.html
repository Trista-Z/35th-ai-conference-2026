Centering on "Human-Machine Symbiosis" , the conference delves into three pillars: Technical Safety addresses risks from LLMs and AI Agents to establish standards for explainable and accountable AI systems; Global Governance leverages the unique "One Country, Two Systems" framework to analyze diverse regulatory paths, positioning Hong Kong as a pivotal hub for international dialogue and a forward-looking testbed for AI governance rules ; and Digital Literacy leverages the IDLP project to integrate technical education with humanistic values, nurturing future talents with critical thinking and ethical awareness. The conference strives to bridge disciplinary gaps and provide comprehensive solutions for a secure and sustainable AI ecosystem.
本次大会以“人机共生”为核心理念 ，深入探讨三大支柱议题：在技术安全方面，聚焦大模型与AI Agent带来的安全风险，致力于构建可解释、可问责的技术标准与风险防范体系；在全球治理层面，发挥香港“一国两制”的制度优势，系统研讨各国治理路径，推动香港成为全球AI规则制定的对话枢纽与前瞻性监管试验田；在数字素养维度，依托IDLP项目将技术教育与人文关怀融合，培养具备批判性思维与伦理意识的新一代人才。大会旨在打破技术、法律与人文壁垒，为构建安全、可持续的AI生态贡献方案。

Hong Kong AI Governance Declaration
人机共生时代AI治理香港宣言

One of the core outcomes of the conference is the release of the **Hong Kong Declaration on AI Governance in the Era of Human-Machine Symbiosis**. Drawing on the insights of leading global scholars, the Declaration puts forward a set of AI governance principles that align with mainstream international values while accommodating the developmental characteristics of Asia. It emphasizes that, while advancing AI technological innovation, fundamental human values must be upheld to ensure that technological progress benefits all humanity. Through the issuance of this landmark document, Hong Kong will demonstrate its responsibility and commitment in the field of technology governance to the international community, and substantially elevate its standing within the global discourse system on AI governance.
大会的核心成果之一是发布《人机共生时代AI治理香港宣言》。该宣言将汇聚全球顶尖学者的智慧，提出一套既符合国际主流价值观，又兼顾亚洲发展特色的AI治理原则。宣言将重点强调在推动AI技术创新的同时，必须坚守人类核心价值，确保技术发展惠及全人类。通过这一宣言的发布，香港将向国际社会展示其在科技治理领域的责任与担当，显著提升其在全球AI治理话语体系中的地位。
